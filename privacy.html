FriendMap Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. What we collect

  • Account: your email address and a password (stored only as a salted hash).
  • Profile: the name, photo, bio, interests and gender you choose to add. Your date of birth, which is used only to confirm that you are an adult and is never shown to anyone.
  • Content: the events, posts, comments, reviews and chat messages you create, and the photos you attach to them. Photos are stripped of their metadata, including location, before upload.
  • Activity: the events you join or save, the groups you belong to, the people you block and the reports you send.
  • Device: a push-notification token, if you allow notifications.

2. Location

FriendMap asks for your location only while you use the app, to show what is happening around you and how far it is. Your position is sent with those requests and is not stored. It is never shared with other users. Events have a location because their organizers set one.

3. What other people can see

Your name, photo, bio, interests, gender, reputation and, if you leave it switched on, when you were last active are visible to signed-in users. Your email, date of birth and notification settings are visible only to you. Chat messages are visible to the participants of the event they belong to.

4. How we use it

To run the service: show events and people to each other, deliver messages and notifications, keep the community safe (reports, blocks, moderation) and prevent abuse. We do not sell your data, show advertising, or track you across other apps and websites.

5. Who processes it for us

  • Supabase (database, authentication, file storage and realtime messaging).
  • Apple (delivery of push notifications).

They act on our instructions and only to provide those services.

6. How long we keep it

For as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, your profile, content, messages and photos are deleted with it. Reports about content that broke the rules may be kept for as long as needed to keep banned users out.

7. Your choices

  • Edit your profile at any time.
  • Hide when you were last active: Settings › Privacy & Security.
  • Turn notifications off in Settings › Notifications or in iOS Settings.
  • Delete your account and data: Settings › Privacy & Security › Delete Account.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, or anything else about it: anzo.silagadze007@gmail.com.

8. Children

FriendMap is not for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from minors.

9. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
